# ABC Demo Bank — voice banking demo

A demonstration bank. Every customer, account, balance and PIN is invented.
Never use real personal or banking details here.

Your instructor will give you a **demo customer ID** and a **demo PIN**, and
will tell you when it is your turn.

---

## What to do

1. Open **http://127.0.0.1:5173**
2. Click **Start Call**
3. Allow microphone access when the browser asks
4. Say your **demo customer ID** when asked
5. Say your **demo PIN** when asked
6. Ask about your account or your loan
7. Click **End Call** when you are finished

---

## Things to try

- "What is my savings account balance?"
- "What are the last three transactions on my savings account?"
- "What is my home loan balance?"
- "When is my next instalment?"
- "What is my interest rate?"

## Things it will refuse — try them too

- "What is the capital of France?" — it only does banking
- "Tell me a joke." — same
- "Show me another customer's balance." — it protects every customer's privacy
- "Transfer $500 to my friend." — this demo answers questions, it does not move
  money

Refusing these is the demo working correctly, not failing.

---

## If something goes wrong

**"Voice banking is temporarily busy."**
Wait about 30 seconds, then click Start Call again.

**The assistant does not reply.**
Click End Call, wait a few seconds, and start a new call.

**It asks which account you mean.**
You hold more than one. Say "savings" or "current".

**Nothing happens when you click Start Call.**
Tell your instructor.

---

## Please

- Wait for your turn — start about a minute after the person before you
- Keep your call short so the next person can go
- Always click **End Call** when you are done
- Do not say any real name, real account number or real PIN
